Empowering Her Today, Building a Better Tomorrow.

Who We Are

Together for Her is a youth-led initiative founded by young people who believe that young people should not only talk about problems in their communities but also take action to create change.

We are a group of young people working to raise awareness, mobilize resources, and create meaningful support for girls and women facing difficult circumstances, particularly those affected by obstetric fistula, social stigma, and lack of access to basic needs and opportunities.

Our work is driven by a simple belief: every girl and woman deserves dignity, support, and the opportunity to rebuild her life.

What We Do

Through Together for Her, we organize youth-led activities, awareness campaigns, fundraising initiatives, community engagement programs, and partnerships with organizations working on women’s and girls’ rights and well-being.

Our Impact on the Community

Although Together for Her is a youth-led and growing initiative, our work has already created opportunities for young people to become actively involved in community service and advocacy.

Youth Engagement & Leadership

  • Mobilize young people to participate in community-focused initiatives.
  • Create opportunities for youth to use their leadership, creativity, and skills to address community challenges.

Awareness & Advocacy

  • Raise awareness about issues affecting girls and women that are often overlooked.
  • Give visibility to the experiences of women and girls affected by issues such as obstetric fistula and social exclusion.

Partnerships & Mobilization

  • Connect with local institutions, organizations, and partners to build collaboration and support.
"Our goal is not simply to organize one event or campaign. We want to build a culture where young people take responsibility for the communities they belong to and work together to create lasting change."
