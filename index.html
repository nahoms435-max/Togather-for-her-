Our Objectives

How we bring tangible change to high school girls and young women.

01. Hygiene Dignity

Supplying safe, sustainable menstrual hygiene products to girls in need across local schools and communities.

02. Educational Advocacy

Conducting workshops to de-stigmatize menstrual health and empower girls with reproductive knowledge.

03. Youth Leadership

Fostering youth-led community programs that empower young women to become leaders and changemakers.
