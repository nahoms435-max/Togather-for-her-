What We Do

Youth-Led Activities

Organizing youth-led initiatives and awareness campaigns across communities to drive organic change.

Empowerment Programs

Building targeted fundraising and community engagement programs that support vulnerable girls.

Strategic Partnerships

Partnering with established organizations focused on women's rights, health, and well-being.

Our Impact on the Community

Youth Engagement and Leadership

  • Mobilizing young people to participate in community-focused initiatives.
  • Creating opportunities for youth to build leadership skills and address social challenges.

Awareness & Advocacy

  • Raising awareness about critical issues affecting women and girls in Ethiopia.
  • Giving visibility to health issues like obstetric fistula and social exclusion.

Partnerships & Mobilization

  • Connecting with local institutions and organizations to build sustainable collaboration.
  • Mobilizing resources and in-kind support for women facing hardship.
